About the tracker

Public LMG1 aircraft records, collected from FlightAware and stored as flight history. This is an independent, unofficial project.

Reading the map

Records can be delayed. Aircraft movements do not confirm who is on board. City markers are approximate; lines connect reported places, not actual flight paths.

Data sources

Map tiles: OpenStreetMap. Airports: OurAirports (public domain). Cities: GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).

Airport and city coordinates are selected and reformatted into local JSON. Uncached places use the configured geocoding service, with results saved in your browser.
